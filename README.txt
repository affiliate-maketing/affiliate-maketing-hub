AFFILIATE MARKETING HUB — WEBSITE STARTER

FILES
- index.html: main website
- styles.css: design and mobile layout
- config.js: WhatsApp and email contact details
- script.js: mobile menu and contact links
- disclosure.html: affiliate disclosure page

FIRST STEPS
1. Extract the ZIP file on your Windows computer.
2. Open the folder.
3. Double-click index.html to preview the website in your browser.
4. Open config.js using Notepad.
5. Replace:
   whatsappNumber: "234XXXXXXXXXX"
   with your WhatsApp number in international format, digits only.
   Example format: 2348012345678
6. Replace your-email@example.com with your real business email address.
7. Save config.js and refresh index.html.

ADD REAL PRODUCTS
The product cards in index.html are sample placeholders. Before advertising:
- Use genuine product photos you have permission to use.
- Add accurate product names and descriptions.
- Add the seller's real product page or your approved affiliate tracking link.
- Check prices, stock and terms with the seller.
- Clearly disclose affiliate links and sponsored promotions.
Never invent reviews, discounts, product claims or endorsements.

PUBLISH FREE WITH GITHUB PAGES
1. Create or sign in to a GitHub account.
2. Create a new public repository, for example affiliate-marketing-hub.
3. Upload index.html, styles.css, config.js, script.js, disclosure.html and README.txt.
   Keep all these files in the repository's top-level folder.
4. In the repository, open Settings → Pages.
5. Under Build and deployment, choose Deploy from a branch.
6. Select the main branch and /(root), then Save.
7. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish. Its website address will appear in the Pages section.
8. Open the published address on your phone and computer to test it.

IMPORTANT
- GitHub Pages websites are public. Do not put passwords, private customer data or secret API keys in these files.
- Your WhatsApp number and business email will be visible to visitors.
- Free hosting providers may change their interface or terms.
- A custom domain is optional and usually costs extra.
- This starter website does not process payments, store customer details, or automatically track affiliate sales.
